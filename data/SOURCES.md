# Sources

All three datasets come from the US DOT Bureau of Transportation Statistics (BTS) and are public domain. The course menu allows a small copy with credit for both; I keep only my own pivot tables in the analysis folder, not the publisher's files.

## barge-rates (course menu ID OP-2)
- Publisher: BTS, Supply Chain and Freight Indicators. The series comes from the U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Marketing Service.
- Link: https://data.bts.gov/d/y5ut-ibwt
- Indicator: Downbound Grain Barge Rates
- Date accessed: 2026-11-23 (publisher's data interface)
- License or terms: Public Domain U.S. Government (https://www.usa.gov/government-works)
- Rows: 2,877 for this indicator; 2,605 have a rate and 272 are blank.

## grain-ports (course menu ID OP-3)
- Publisher: BTS, Port Data (National Transportation Atlas Database)
- Link: https://data.bts.gov/d/5rpz-kgm9
- Date accessed: 2026-11-23 (publisher's data interface)
- License or terms: Public Domain (BTS portal metadata)
- Rows: 8,386 in the full table; 157 rows of corn and soybeans in the top 5 food and farm commodities, 2020 to 2023.

## diesel-prices (course menu ID OP-2, second indicator)
- Publisher: BTS, Supply Chain and Freight Indicators. The series comes from the U.S. Energy Information Administration.
- Link: https://data.bts.gov/d/y5ut-ibwt
- Indicator: U.S. Diesel Sales Prices (in Dollars per Gallon)
- Date accessed: 2026-11-23 (publisher's data interface)
- License or terms: Public Domain U.S. Government
- Rows: 411 weekly rows, 2019-01-07 to 2026-11-16, none blank.
